# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-22


- New block: **Brass Anchor**, keeps chunks loaded and ticking (machines, crops, animals) without a player nearby.
- Right-click to change the radius (1×1 up to 5×5 by default), sneak-right-click for status, redstone turns it off.
- Rides along on Create contraptions and trains and loads the chunks around its current position.
- Sable support: an anchor on a ship keeps the ship loaded (also across restarts) and loads its surroundings.
- Made with AI assistance (Claude by Anthropic), including code, textures, 3D model and logo.
- Engineer's Goggles tooltip, `/brassanchor list` admin command, server config with radius and per-dimension limits.
